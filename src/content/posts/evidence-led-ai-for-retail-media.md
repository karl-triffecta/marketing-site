<p>The real question a brand or agency should ask is simpler: can you see why the AI recommended that change, and can you prove what happened next?</p>

<p>Our approach follows one clear path: Evidence → Recommendation → Controlled action.</p>

<ul>
  <li>

  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Recommendation:</strong> the AI proposes the next best move, from budget shifts to bid changes, with the reasoning attached
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Controlled action:</strong> your team approves, and every change is logged in an audit trail you can review at any time
  </li>
</ul>

<p>No black box. No "trust us". If a client, a finance director or a retailer asks why spend moved on Tuesday, you have the answer.</p>

<h2>Day-parting you control</h2>

<p>Shoppers do not buy evenly across the day, so spend should not be spread evenly either. Triffecta's AI and day-parting align content, media and spend with peak customer opportunity across connected retailers.</p>

<p>Crucially, the day-parting rules belong to you. You set the windows, the limits and the priorities. The AI works within them.</p>

<h2>Human + AI, properly defined</h2>

<p>We are clear about who does what. AI handles discovery, reasoning and the repetitive operational work that eats your team's week. People keep the strategy, the controls, the approvals and the spend decisions.</p>

<p>That is not a compromise. It is how you scale retail media without losing grip on your budget or your client relationships.</p>
