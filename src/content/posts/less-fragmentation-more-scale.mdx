<h2>Where most teams are today</h2>

<p>
  If you run retail media for a brand or an agency, this will look familiar:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Separate log-ins and workflows for every retail media network</li>
  <li>The same campaign built again and again: per brand, per SKU, per retailer</li>
  <li>Performance checked campaign by campaign, console by console</li>
  <li>Reporting stitched together from CSV and XLS exports, with no consistent cross-retailer view</li>
</ul>

<p>
